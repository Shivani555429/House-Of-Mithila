about us
Makhana cultivation requires dedication, skill, and hard work. Farmers spend long hours in water-filled ponds managing the crop and harvesting the produce.This traditional farming practice supports thousands of rural families and plays a significant role in the agricultural economy of Bihar and the Mithila region.
Pure Makhana

Makhana, also known as Fox Nut is a highly nutritious aquatic crop grown mainly in the ponds and wetlands of the Mithila region. Rich in protein, fiber, and antioxidants, it is widely consumed as a healthy snack. The seeds are carefully processed to create the light, crunchy makhana enjoyed worldwide.

Healthy Makhana

Fresh makhana seeds are the foundation of premium-quality fox nuts. After collection, the seeds undergo several stages including cleaning, grading, roasting, and popping. Proper handling and processing ensure superior taste, texture, and nutritional value, making makhana a popular choice among health-conscious consumers.

Fresh Makhana

Harvesting makhana seeds is a crucial step in the production process. Farmers collect the mature seeds from water bodies and separate them from the fruits. These seeds are then cleaned, dried, and prepared for further processing. The quality of harvested seeds directly influences the final quality of the popped makhana.
